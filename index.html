WHISPER COURT

online deduction for 2–6 players

offline

Secrets travel faster than crowns.

A tiny online deduction game. Keep one card, draw one card, play one card. Read the table, mislead your rivals, and be the last courtier standing.

Rooms use Firebase Realtime Database, so friends can connect reliably across different Wi-Fi networks, mobile data, schools, and countries. Anonymous sign-in keeps every player’s private hand isolated.

Original game implementation inspired by compact social-deduction card games. No assets required.

How to win

On your turn, draw one and play one. Be the final surviving player, or hold the highest-value card when the deck empties. First to the required number of seals wins.
